Data Processing Addendum — Brightdesk Inc. ("Brightdesk") and Customer. Synthetic sample.

1. Scope. This Addendum applies to Customer Personal Data processed by Brightdesk. The subject-matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are listed in Schedule 1.

2. Instructions. Brightdesk shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer as set out in the Agreement. Brightdesk will inform Customer if it believes an instruction infringes applicable data protection law.

3. Security. Brightdesk maintains technical and organisational measures designed to protect Customer Personal Data, as described in its Security Overview.

4. Sub-processors. Customer provides general authorisation for Brightdesk to engage sub-processors. Brightdesk will update the list of sub-processors posted on its website from time to time. Brightdesk shall impose data protection obligations on sub-processors that are no less protective than this Addendum and remains liable for their acts and omissions.

5. Data subjects. Brightdesk will provide reasonable assistance to Customer in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights, at Customer's cost.

6. Security incidents. Brightdesk will notify Customer of a breach affecting Customer Personal Data within five (5) business days of confirming the incident.

7. Return and deletion. Upon termination of the Agreement, Brightdesk will delete Customer Personal Data, except that Brightdesk may retain copies in backups and archives in accordance with its standard practices.

8. Audits. Customer may audit Brightdesk's compliance once per year. Brightdesk's then-current SOC 2 Type II report shall satisfy Customer's audit rights in lieu of any on-site inspection.

9. Transfers. Customer Personal Data may be processed in the United States. Transfers are made under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated by reference.

Schedule 1. Subject-matter: customer support ticketing. Duration: term of the Agreement. Nature and purpose: storing and routing support conversations. Types of personal data: name, email, message content. Categories of data subjects: Customer's end users and staff.
